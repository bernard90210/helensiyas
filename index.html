Who we are

Every great story has the power to move hearts, challenge perspectives and shape society.

Founded in 2007, Helensiyas Productions is an independent creative production house dedicated to the art of meaningful storytelling — across film, television and literary publishing.

For nearly two decades we have developed content that goes beyond entertainment. We seek stories that matter: culturally relevant, emotionally engaging and socially impactful. Whether on screen or in print, our goal is work that leaves a lasting impression.

Driven by creativity, integrity and purpose, we collaborate with talented artists, writers and filmmakers who share our vision of storytelling as a force for good.
